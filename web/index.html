Sound is analysed on this device and then discarded. Nothing is uploaded. After your first visit Hum also works with no internet connection.

One person's sounds, laid out by how alike they are

Each dot is one vocalization. Dots that sit together sound alike to Hum. Colour is the meaning the caregiver gave. Where colours separate, Hum can tell meanings apart; where they mix, it will say it is not sure.

Communication passport

A page to hand to a new teacher, nurse, or sitter. Add what helps in your own words, then print it.

Hand this voice to another device

Save the voice as a small file and send it to the new caregiver. They open Hum, load the file, and it understands this person straight away. The file holds numbers that describe the sounds, not recordings.
